Sensitivity analysis on the external convection coefficient h_env
==================================================================

Reviewer point 3 (round 2): the effect of the external convection
coefficient on the main results was tested by re-running the long-term
LF=0.95 ammonia simulation with h_env varied between 0.5 x and 2.0 x the
baseline value of 14.839 W m^-2 K^-1, while keeping all other parameters
(U_L, U_V, U_b, geometry, perlite properties) at the values used in the
manuscript. The Robin boundary condition at the external tank wall is the
only quantity changed.

Results (LF = 0.95, ammonia, P = 101 325 Pa)

  Time         h_env x 0.5     baseline      h_env x 2.0
                7.4195         14.8390        29.6780  W m^-2 K^-1
  ----------------------------------------------------------
  Q_tot at  1 h     106.30        106.30        106.30  kW
  Q_tot at 24 h      30.85         30.87         30.88  kW
  Q_tot at 72 h      28.90         28.97         29.01  kW
  Q_tot at 504 h     28.89         28.97         29.01  kW
  BOG at 504 h       75.96         76.16         76.26  kg h^-1
  <T_w> at 504 h    257.70        257.81        257.86  K

Quasi-steady relative changes vs baseline:
  h_env x 0.5 :  Q_tot -0.26 % ,  BOG -0.26 %
  h_env x 2.0 :  Q_tot +0.14 % ,  BOG +0.14 %

Maximum wall-temperature deviation across the entire 3-week time history:
  |T_w(0.5 h_env) - T_w(baseline)| max = 0.42 K
  |T_w(2.0 h_env) - T_w(baseline)| max = 0.21 K

Interpretation
--------------
The total thermal resistance of the wall is dominated by the perlite
insulation (R_perlite ~ r_o ln(r_o/r_i)/k_p approx 11.3 m^2 K W^-1
referred to the outer area), whereas the external convective resistance
1/h_env approx 0.067 m^2 K W^-1 contributes ~0.6 % of the total. Halving
or doubling h_env therefore shifts U_L by less than 0.6 % and the
quasi-steady heat ingress and BOG by under 0.3 %. The transient is also
robust: peak heat ingress at t = 1 h is identical to within 0.01 % across
the three cases because the wall is initially at T_env and the inner
boundary controls the fast transient. The largest single deviation
observed in the wall temperature distribution is 0.42 K, located near
the outer surface during the first day of the 0.5 x run, and is
indistinguishable on the scale of the published Figs. 2 and 3.

Supporting figures
------------------
  Results/Figures/Fig_2_h_sensitivity.svg
  Results/Figures/Fig_3_h_sensitivity.svg

Solid coloured lines reproduce the published baseline run; dotted lines
are the 0.5 x case and dashed lines the 2.0 x case. The three sets of
curves are visually superimposed throughout, confirming that the choice
of h_env does not affect the conclusions of the paper.
